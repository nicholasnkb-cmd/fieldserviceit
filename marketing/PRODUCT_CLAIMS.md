## Claims requiring evidence before use

- Time or money saved.
- Setup, onboarding, or migration duration.
- Customer count, outcomes, testimonials, retention, or traction.
- Uptime, SLA, RPO, RTO, response time, or load capacity.
- Market size/share, competitor price, or feature superiority.
- Number or production depth of RMM integrations.
- “Enterprise-grade,” “audit-ready,” “compliant,” or “certified.”
- “Unlimited” usage without defined technical, contractual, and fair-use limits.
- Support hours or response guarantees.
- Accounting sync, native mobile, on-premises, or payment methods absent from the current catalog and support policy.

## Prohibited claims at present

- “SOC 2 certified” or “SOC 2 audit in progress.”
- “HIPAA compliant” or “HIPAA BAA available.”
- “Customers save 60%,” “15+ hours per week,” or “$2,400+ per technician.”
- “Setup takes 45 minutes.”
- “More RMM integrations than any competitor.”
- “Production-ready” without the exact release and completed environment evidence.
- Credit-card, ACH, or wire acceptance while billing is PayPal-only.

## Approved positioning

> FieldserviceIT connects service requests, assets, technicians, field work, and RMM context in one tenant-aware operations workspace.

> Keep the RMM you prefer and improve the governed customer workflow around it.
